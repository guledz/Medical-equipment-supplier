About us

A direct source for medical equipment in Addis Ababa

Yonas Merga Medical Equipment supplies mobility, rehabilitation and home-care equipment to individuals, families, caregivers and institutions across Ethiopia. We have built our business through online marketplaces, and this website exists to give customers a direct relationship with us — clear information, a real person to talk to, and no guesswork.

We do not publish specifications, certifications or claims we have not verified. Where a detail is not yet confirmed, we say so plainly rather than guess. This is a deliberate choice: we would rather be honest about what we don't yet know than present something as fact when it isn't.

If you are choosing equipment for yourself or someone you care for, we are glad to talk it through with you — by phone or WhatsApp — before you decide.

Please note. We are equipment suppliers, not medical professionals. Nothing on this site is medical advice. We recommend consulting a qualified healthcare professional about your specific condition or needs.

What we stand for

  • Accuracy. We publish what we know to be true, and label what we don't.

  • Transparency. Prices, availability and specifications are confirmed openly, not hidden.

  • Respect. Plain language, no pressure, no exaggerated claims.

  • Reliability. Someone is reachable to answer your questions, before and after purchase.

No. Prices shown are indicative and are confirmed with you at the time of enquiry, before any purchase is agreed.
